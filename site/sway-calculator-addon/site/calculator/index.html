How this fits with the Atlases

EPR Atlas answers whether a brand is in scope and what the general rates are. The Compostable Atlas answers whether a pack can be labeled compostable and how each state treats compostables. This calculator answers one question: what switching a SKU to Sway film changes per unit, state by state.

Open questions that change results: whether California’s surcharges apply to the whole film or its plastic share; whether seaweed polysaccharides count as plastic; how the organic-input pathway resolves after June 30, 2027; and which Oregon category fits a blended film.
